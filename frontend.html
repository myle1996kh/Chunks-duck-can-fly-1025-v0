📊 WPM Calculation Method

How WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated:

  1. Spectral Analysis: Analyzes audio frequency content using FFT (Fast Fourier Transform)
  2. Syllable Detection: Finds peaks in spectral centroid to identify potential syllables
  3. Speech Filtering: Only counts syllables during actual speech segments (not silence)
  4. Conversion: Converts syllables to words using 1.5 syllables per word ratio
  5. WPM Calculation: (Syllables per second ÷ 1.5) × 60

Why this method is accurate:

  • • Uses advanced signal processing instead of simple time estimation
  • • Accounts for speech pauses and silence periods
  • • Provides detailed syllable detection metrics
  • • More reliable than duration-based calculations
